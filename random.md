The five behaviors, in the user's words


Jobs tab → "By client" toggle — jobs fold around the client entity instead of a flat list.
Click a client (e.g., Ramesh LLC) — the card expands to show ALL documents for that case: processed ones AND requested-but-not-yet-received ones.
Click a document row (e.g., the W-9) — drill into that job: the document, extracted fields, statuses, and the source overlay (reuse the existing extract/review view — do not build a second one).
Click a schedule tag (e.g., "⏰ Encompass — daily pull") — a right-side drawer opens to view/edit that schedule: cadence, scope, pause, run once, recent runs.
Click "✨ Ask across documents" — a right-side conversational drawer opens, scoped to that client's case only.
